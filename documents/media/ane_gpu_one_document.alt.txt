Architecture diagram, "What happens to one document". Three zones left to right plus the GPU. Host, per document: a Document node (ISDA Schedule, 9,696 tokens) splits into 44 windows, and "Embed each window" turns 256 tokens into a 256 x 1024 fp16 block plus RoPE tables and a mask. On the ANE: a dashed "Adapter slot, IS3" (9.2 MB, bound in 90 ms) feeds the resident "Qwen3-0.6B base" (28 blocks, 29 ms per window), which returns hidden states. Host, read and select: a "Score head" turns the hidden states into 12 scores per window; the highlighted "Pick and slice" node keeps the top 3 windows per question plus keyword windows, 17 of 44, and sends only their text with the 12 questions to the "27B" on the GPU (reads 4,862 of 9,696 tokens, 13.1 s). Its 12 answers go to "Answers with sources", where the host cites, for each answer, the highest-scoring sent window that contains it; 14.6 s end to end.
